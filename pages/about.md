# About P64

P64 is a weekly reading group on agentic AI. The name is **P** for papers plus **0x64**, which is 100 in hexadecimal. The group's goal is to read a hundred papers together, and the badge in the top-left corner counts the papers read so far in hex (P00, P01, … P64) and turns from dark red toward crimson as the count grows.

## Why

Agentic AI is moving faster than any one person can read. A shared, regular reading habit gives each of us a steady stream of papers we would otherwise skip, and the written record (summaries and open questions) lets anyone, including people who were not in the room, find out what a paper claims and what we doubted about it.

## First theme: multi-agent systems

We start with multi-agent systems built from large language models: several LLM agents that talk, debate, divide work or play against each other. The candidate list comes from {{N_VENUES}} top venues (see [Method](method.html)) and has {{N_PAPERS}} papers. Anyone may also present a paper outside the list.

## How a meeting works

- **When:** every Thursday, 16:00–17:00, starting 5 November 2026.
- **Before the meeting:** each participant reads one paper of their choice.
- **In the meeting:** 10 minutes per person plus Q&A. No slides are needed; a whiteboard is fine. If many people join, up to four present each week on a rotating basis.
- **At the end:** we pre-register who reads what for next week, and that is what we discuss next time.
- **After the meeting:** a short summary of each paper, what we learned and the questions we left open are added to this site as one page per week ([Weeks](weeks.html)).

## What is public and what is not

This site publishes the paper list, the summaries and the questions. Audio recordings of the meetings (audio only) and detailed minutes are shared with participants only. Consent for both is collected at the meeting.
